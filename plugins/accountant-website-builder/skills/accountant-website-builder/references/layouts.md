# Layouts

All three share the same sections: fixed nav with the main button, hero, moving services strip, "what working with us looks like" (stats and three service cards), three steps with a media panel, a closing line with the main button, and a footer.

| Layout | Hero | Best for | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| `split` | Headline left, tall rounded media panel right, two stats under the button | Traditional practices, partners who want to look established | Calm, considered, serif headings |
| `card` | Full-width rounded media card, headline over the left of it | Bookkeepers and modern firms with one strong image or video | Clean, bright, product-like |
| `full` | Edge-to-edge media, headline bottom left over a dark fade | Niche firms with a big promise (contractors, tax, payroll) | Bold, cinematic, dark |

**Type pairings that work for finance:**
- Fraunces 400 + Inter (trust, traditional)
- Instrument Serif 400 + Inter (editorial, premium)
- Inter Tight 500 or Manrope 700 + Inter (modern, product)
- Space Grotesk 500 + Inter (tech-forward payroll and cloud firms)

**Colour:** one accent only. Dark layouts need light `ink`; check contrast on the hero text over the media. With no media, pick `glow` colours that sit near the accent, not the accent three times.

**Motion that's already built in:** a staggered blur-up entrance on the hero, a slow drifting gradient (no media), reveal-on-scroll for every section, count-up stats, hover lift on cards, and a rotating arrow on buttons. All of it switches off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
