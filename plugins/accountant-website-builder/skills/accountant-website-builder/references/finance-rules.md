# Rules for finance firm websites

From HandL Agency's SEO and website work for UK accountants and bookkeepers.

## Getting found (Google and AI search)
1. **The H1 says the service and the town.** "Accountants in Bristol", not "Welcome to Smith & Co". It's the first thing Google reads to work out what the page is about. `build.py` checks it.
2. **The page title leads with the same phrase**, 65 characters max: "Accountants in Bristol | Fenwick & Lowe".
3. **The first paragraph answers the question.** What you do, where and for whom, before your history. AI answers quote the top of the page.
4. **One page per service and per town you want to rank for.** This skill builds the homepage. Build the others the same way, with their own H1.
5. **What other sites say counts.** Point the user at their institute listing, Google Business Profile and reviews. A website can't fix that on its own.

## Turning visitors into enquiries
6. **One action, everywhere.** The same main button in the nav, the hero and the closing section.
7. **Show a face.** People buy from people. Replace the media panel with the real team as soon as photos exist.
8. **Show prices or a starting price.** Visitors who can't see a figure leave to find one.
9. **Answer the fear.** Most clients who leave an accountant say they never heard from them. Promises like "a monthly call" and "48-hour replies" answer that directly.
10. **Never invent proof.** No made-up reviews, client numbers, logos or awards. Use real ones, or none.

## Compliance
11. Add the firm's regulatory details in the footer before going live: registered name and number, registered office, and its supervisory body (ICAEW, ACCA, AAT, ICB or CIOT as applicable).
12. Cookie consent, a privacy notice and the firm's terms if any forms or tracking go on the page.
