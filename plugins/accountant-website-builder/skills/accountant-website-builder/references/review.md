# Review rubric (step 5)

Score each line 1 to 5 from the screenshots. Fix the three lowest, build, shoot again. Stop at all 4+ or after three rounds.

| # | Check | 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First screen | Within 3 seconds a stranger knows the service, the town and what to click |
| 2 | H1 and title | Both say "[service] in [town]" and still read like a person wrote them |
| 3 | Hierarchy | One headline, one subline, one main button. Nothing competing |
| 4 | Contrast | Hero text readable over the media on desktop and mobile |
| 5 | Media | Real, sharp, on-brand. No stock handshakes, no empty boxes |
| 6 | Typography | Two families max, tight heading letter-spacing, no orphaned single words |
| 7 | Rhythm | Generous, even spacing; sections feel separate |
| 8 | Motion | Entrance and reveals feel smooth, never slow or busy |
| 9 | Trust | Promises are specific and true; no invented proof |
| 10 | Mobile | No horizontal scroll, button visible without scrolling, text 16px+ |

Common fixes:
- Headline wraps badly: shorten it or move the `<br>`.
- Text unreadable on media: darken the fade, or switch to the `split` layout.
- Looks generic: change the type pairing, then the accent, before touching the layout.
